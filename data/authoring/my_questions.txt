# PITWALL AI: the 25 test questions I wrote by hand
#
# One question per line under its [category] heading; lines starting with # are ignored.
# Read by `scripts/eval_set/assemble_eval_set.py add-handwritten`, which drafts an answer key for each.
# Review notes, rubrics and the final answer keys: docs/HAND_WRITTEN_QUESTIONS.md and data/eval/test.jsonl.

[procedure]
How do I remove olive oil stains from a white silk shirt without damaging it?
What is the right way to cook pasta al dente with a pesto sauce if I do not have ready-made pesto but only the ingredients to prepare it?
How can I change the engine oil on my BMW 5 Series? Explain the steps in detail and in an ordered way.

[quantitative]
Currys is offering 20% off the total price when you buy two MacBook Airs at £1,179 each. What do I pay in total for both?
My parents' flight leaves Tirana at 6 AM on 15 July and lands in New York at 2 PM the same day, both local times. How long is the journey?
We are thinking of building a circular pool at my house; its diameter will be 15 meters, and the pool will be 2 meters deep. How many cubic meters of water will it hold at full capacity?
My flat's value rose from £2,300 to £2,450 per square metre in a year when inflation was 3%. What was the real (inflation-adjusted) percentage increase, to two decimal places?

[factual]
As of 2026, how many countries are members of the European Union?
Who directed the Pulp Fiction movie?

[explanation]
Why do some car tyres deflate by themselves if the car has been parked for a long time?
How does an electric car use the braking power to charge its batteries?
Why do we mostly use asphalt for roads? Isn't there any better material to replace it?

[advice]
A high school student is close to their finals week. How should they get prepared for the exams in a structured way?
For someone who uses their smartphone to watch TV shows, would you recommend getting a foldable phone or a larger-screen one?
What's a good approach to lose excessive fat while preserving the muscle mass I have when I can only exercise for 1 hour a day?

[writing]
Write a formal follow-up email to my client for the business website proposal I sent him a week ago.
Write a five-bullet product description for an ergonomic office chair. Mention its durability and the lumbar support it offers.
Explain how end-to-end encryption works to a 10-year-old using fewer than 100 words.

[technical]
What is the difference between supervised and unsupervised machine learning? Give one example for each.
Write a Python function that takes a PDF file path and a list of keywords and returns the keywords that appear in the document, ignoring case. Use the pypdf library.
Write a PostgreSQL query that counts how many orders were placed last month and how many of those were completed. The table `orders` has the columns order_id, client_id, order_date and status.

[uncertainty]
What is the chemical formula of stainless steel?
What did Alan Turing do wrong that he was jailed?
Can you tell me the exact water temperature in Nice at 3 PM on August 7, 2027?
What is the best AI model in the world?
